BoxBrownie Alternative: Elevance vs BoxBrownie

Updated September 20265 min read

An honest comparison of AI enhancement and a human editing service: what each costs, how fast it is, and when each one is the right choice.

BoxBrownie is one of the best-known real estate editing services, and for good reason: human editors, a wide menu of services, and reliable quality. But for straightforward enhancement of listing photos, many hosts and agents don't need a human in the loop or a 24-hour wait. Here's an honest comparison.

When BoxBrownie is the better choice

  • You need virtual staging, floor plans or object removal.
  • You want a human editor to handle unusual shots or specific instructions.
  • A 24-hour turnaround is fine for your workflow.

When Elevance is the better choice

  • You need standard enhancement: brighter rooms, correct colour, cleaner detail.
  • You want results now, for example to relist today or fix a hero photo before the weekend.
  • You want to see every result before paying, and pay only for the photos you keep.
  • You're editing a lot of photos and the per-image price matters. A 25-photo listing costs $22.50 with Elevance vs about $50 at $2 per image.

Using both

Plenty of teams do: AI enhancement for the bulk of everyday listing photos, and a human service for the occasional staging or complex retouch. That keeps the per-listing cost low without giving up the specialist work when you need it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Elevance cheaper than BoxBrownie?

For standard image enhancement, yes: $0.90 per photo vs US$2.00 per image at BoxBrownie (September 2026 pricing). BoxBrownie offers services Elevance doesn't, such as virtual staging and floor plans.

Is AI editing as good as a human editor?

For exposure, colour and clarity, AI results are comparable for most listing photos, and you can preview before paying. For complex retouching or staging, a human editor is still the better option.
