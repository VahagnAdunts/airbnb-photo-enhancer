What the AI fixes in every photo

  • Exposure and dark rooms: lifts shadows so bedrooms and bathrooms don't look like caves, without blowing out windows.
  • White balance: removes the yellow, blue or green casts from mixed bulbs, so every room in your gallery matches.
  • Colour and clarity: natural, not oversaturated; sharper detail on textures like bedding and wood.
  • Dull exteriors and views: brings back colour in balcony, pool and window views that phones wash out.
  • Day-to-night: turns a daytime exterior into a warm evening shot with the interior lights on.
  • Light styling: can add small finishing touches like a throw or cushions. Check each result and only use photos that match what guests will find.

Why photo editing matters on Airbnb

Guests decide whether to tap your listing from a thumbnail in search results. Then they judge the property on the first five photos. A dark or yellow hero shot loses the click before your price, reviews or description are even seen. Good editing doesn't misrepresent your place. It makes the photos look the way the room feels in person.

Most hosts don't need a new shoot. They need their existing photos edited properly and consistently. That's the job Elevance does.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Airbnb photo editing cost with Elevance?

$0.90 per photo you download. Previews are free, there is no subscription, and credits never expire. A typical 25-photo listing costs $22.50.

Will the photos look fake or over-edited?

No. The AI corrects exposure, white balance and clarity so the photo looks like the room does in person. It may add small styling touches such as a throw or cushions; you preview every result and only download the ones you're happy with.

Is editing listing photos allowed on Airbnb?

Yes. Improving lighting, colour and straightness is standard practice. Photos should still accurately represent the space, so avoid edits that hide defects or add features that don't exist.

Can I use the edited photos on Booking.com and VRBO?

Yes. Downloads are high-quality JPGs with no watermark, and you can use them on any platform or your own website.

Do I need a professional camera?

No. Most of our users shoot on an iPhone or Android phone. Shoot during daylight with lights on and curtains open, then let the AI handle the edit.
